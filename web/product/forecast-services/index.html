Which forecast fits your decision?

34 weather and climate forecasting approaches, positioned by planning horizon, spatial extent and variable impact proximity.

How to read this guide
  • Position. Each sphere is stretched along each axis to the planning horizon, spatial extent and impact proximity that approach typically covers. The planning horizon and spatial extent axes are logarithmic.
  • Placement and maturity. These are Klimatikal's assessments as of 14 November 2026 and will change as evidence does. Established means routine in operational use; experimental means early, limited or still being verified.
  • Skill evidence. Figures are quoted from the cited sources for the metric, region and period each used. They are not comparable across approaches. Where a source is a poster, preprint or vendor material, the entry says so, and where nothing was found for Indonesia, it says that too.
  • Scope. This is a guide to choosing an approach, not a forecast, and not a recommendation for any specific decision.
