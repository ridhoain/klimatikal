Privacy

What we collect, why, and who handles it. Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version

We collect only what you choose to send us through the contact form. We use it to reply to you. We do not sell your information, and we do not add advertising or analytics tools to this website.

The contact form

When you request a consultation we receive your email address, the subject and the message you type. We use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. The message is delivered by email to our mailbox. We keep it for as long as we need it to deal with your request, and you can ask us to delete it.

The form has a human check provided by Cloudflare Turnstile, loaded only on the contact page. To tell people from automated programs it may process technical information about your visit, such as your IP address and browser details, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Visiting the website

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page you request so that the site loads and stays secure. We do not set cookies. We do not run advertising or analytics tools.

The weather map loads its mapping software from unpkg.com, a public code delivery service, so that service sees your IP address when your browser fetches it. Weather data comes from our own data address, data.klimatikal.com.

The Atlas Cuaca pages remember your language choice (Indonesian or English) in your own browser. That setting stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Who handles your information

We use these services to run the site and answer your messages: Cloudflare (hosting and the human check), Resend (sending the form message as email) and Zoho Mail (our mailbox). They act on our behalf, and they may process data in countries other than where you live.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us through the contact form and we will reply.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date.
