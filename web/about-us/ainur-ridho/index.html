About us

Ainur Ridho

Climate scientist

ainur.ridho@klimatikal.com

I am a climate scientist specialising in climate hazard and vulnerability assessment, including hazardous weather forecasting and climate modelling. My work combines a philosophically grounded approach with analytical tools to synthesise knowledge and insights.

I have collaborated with government agencies and organisations, contributing to policy-making in disaster risk reduction and sustainability, from planning and monitoring to evaluation. I am particularly keen on translating complex weather and climate information into actionable decisions.
